GTA 6 Vehicles in Leonida: Cars, Bikes, Boats and Aircraft

Last checked: October 14, 2026 | Fan guide, not affiliated with Rockstar Games

Vehicles have always been the backbone of Grand Theft Auto, and GTA 6 looks ready to deliver the biggest and most varied garage in series history. Between the two official trailers and Rockstar's press materials, we have already seen sports cars, muscle cars, motorcycles, speedboats, jet skis, airboats, helicopters, and even a cargo plane. With the game set across the state of Leonida, a fictional version of Florida, land, sea, and air travel all matter more than ever.

This guide collects everything Rockstar has actually shown or confirmed about GTA 6 vehicles, plus informed reading of what the map demands. If you want the full reference as it keeps growing, the GTA 6 vehicles guide tracks each confirmed vehicle type in one place.

Cars: sports, muscle, and everyday traffic

Trailer 1 and trailer 2 both show a wide spread of road vehicles. Sports cars tear along Ocean Drive-style strips in Vice City, muscle cars cruise the highways, and lowriders bounce through neighborhood scenes. Rockstar has not published an official vehicle list, so exact model names are unconfirmed, but the trailers clearly show sedans, SUVs, pickup trucks, convertibles, and classic cars parked on the streets of Vice City.

Expect the usual GTA mix of drivable traffic cars plus rare finds. In past games, certain cars only spawned in specific neighborhoods or at certain times of day, and Leonida's size suggests that pattern will continue.

Motorcycles and bikes

Bikes get solid screen time in both trailers. We see choppers, sports bikes, and dirt bikes, including off-road riding that hints at countryside areas beyond Vice City proper. Leonida's rural regions, swamps, and beaches give bikes a real role: weaving through Vice City traffic, cutting across dirt trails, or cruising the coastal highways.

One scene shows characters riding side by side on open road. That is speculation about co-op style free-roam moments, not confirmed, but the footage itself confirms bikes are fully drivable.

Boats, jet skis, and airboats

This is where Leonida's Florida setting changes the game. The trailers show speedboats, jet skis, yachts, and fan boats (airboats) gliding through swampy waterways that look like the Everglades. Water vehicles were always fun in GTA, but in a state with this much coastline and swamp, they become genuinely important for getting around.

The second trailer shows Lucia on a boat and several watercraft scenes that suggest boating missions or activities. Given how much of Leonida appears to be water or wetlands, owning or stealing a good boat may matter as much as owning a fast car. Check the GTA 6 map of Leonida to see just how much of the state is coastline, keys, and swamp where boats beat cars.

Aircraft: helicopters, planes, and skydiving

Helicopters appear in both trailers, including police choppers, which confirms the wanted system will chase you into the sky as always. A cargo plane makes an appearance too, and one shot shows skydiving, a series staple since GTA 5. Seaplanes fit the setting perfectly, though none have been clearly confirmed yet.

With a map this large, aircraft will likely be the fastest way to cross Leonida. Expect flight schools or similar activities to return, and expect the skies over Vice City to get busy once players get their hands on helicopters.

Will GTA 6 have flying cars or the Oppressor?

Nothing in the trailers or official materials shows flying cars, rocket bikes, or anything like GTA Online's Oppressor Mk II. Those belong to GTA Online's later updates. For the base GTA 6 story experience, expect grounded, realistic vehicles. Whether a future GTA Online iteration brings back the wilder machines is unconfirmed.

Are there boats you can buy and store?

Rockstar has not confirmed a boat purchase or storage system. GTA 5 let players buy docks and boats, so a similar system is plausible, but treat it as a rumor until Rockstar says so. What is confirmed is that many boats are drivable in the trailers.

Will vehicle customization return?

Not officially confirmed, but extremely likely. Customization has been core to GTA since San Andreas, and the car culture scenes in the trailers strongly suggest players will be able to modify their rides. Details on shops, parts, and costs are unknown.

How big a role will water vehicles play?

A bigger one than in any previous GTA. Leonida's geography, with its keys, coastline, and swamp regions, makes boats and jet skis practical transport, not just toys. The trailers dedicate real screen time to watercraft, which signals Rockstar knows this.
