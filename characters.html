GTA 6 Characters: Jason Duval, Lucia Caminos, and the Leonida Cast

Last checked: October 14, 2026 | Fan guide, not affiliated with Rockstar Games

For the first time in series history, Grand Theft Auto 6 gives us two protagonists in a modern setting: Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos. Rockstar confirmed both leads through the trailers and the official trailer 2 press release in May 2025. Their story, a crime saga set across Leonida, is the heart of GTA 6, and the supporting cast revealed so far paints a vivid picture of the state they are trying to survive in.

This guide covers every character Rockstar has officially confirmed, what we know about each of them, and what remains rumor. For the complete roster with images, see the full GTA 6 characters reference.

Lucia Caminos: fresh out and hungry

Lucia Caminos is the first female protagonist in a mainline GTA game, and Rockstar has given her a sharp backstory. According to the official trailer 2 press materials, she has just been released from Leonida Penitentiary and is determined to get the good life her mother dreamed of. She is smart, driven, and done with losing.

The first trailer opens with Lucia in a prison jumpsuit talking to a parole officer, which sets up her arc: she did her time, and now she wants out of the cycle. Trailer 2 shows her working jobs with Jason, from small-time scores to bigger ambitions. Lucia reads as the planner of the pair, the one with a plan to finally make it.

Jason Duval: the drifter who found something worth keeping

Jason Duval wants an easy life, but life keeps getting in the way. Per Rockstar's official character descriptions, he grew up around grifters and criminals, served in the army, and now does odd jobs around the Leonida Keys trying to stay out of trouble. Meeting Lucia changes everything.

Jason comes across as the more grounded, physical half of the duo. Where Lucia has the ambition, Jason has the skills and the local knowledge. Their dynamic, described by Rockstar as two people relying on each other when a simple score goes wrong, drives the whole story. Rumors about which actors play Jason and Lucia circulate online, but Rockstar has not confirmed any voice cast, so treat casting claims as unverified.

The supporting cast confirmed in trailer 2

Rockstar's May 2025 press release introduced several supporting characters, and each one hints at a corner of Leonida's criminal world:

Cal Hampton, Jason's friend, a conspiracy-minded slacker who hangs around and seems to know a little too much about everything. Boobie Ike, a Vice City legend who turned street hustle into a legitimate empire spanning real estate, a strip club, and a recording studio. Dre'Quan Priest, a music hustler working with Boobie and chasing a hit record. Raul Bautista, a seasoned bank robber who recruits the crew for high-stakes scores. Brian Heder, a classic drug runner from the Keys era who lets Jason live in one of his properties in exchange for help with shakedowns.

Together they suggest a story that moves between street-level crime, the music business, and big heists, all classic GTA territory with a Leonida flavor.

How the two-protagonist system may work

Rockstar has not detailed the switching mechanics, but GTA 5's character switching is the obvious template. Expect to swap between Jason and Lucia outside missions, with each having their own contacts, safehouses, and story threads that converge on shared heists. The GTA 6 story page tracks confirmed plot details as Rockstar reveals them.

What is confirmed is the premise: a simple score goes wrong, and the two end up on the wrong side of what Rockstar calls the sunniest place in America, counting on each other to survive. Everything beyond that, including mission counts and endings, is unconfirmed.

Who are the GTA 6 protagonists?

Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos, confirmed by Rockstar in the trailers and the official trailer 2 press release. Lucia is the series' first female lead protagonist in a mainline entry.

Is the voice cast confirmed?

No. Rockstar has not announced any voice actors for GTA 6. Names circulating on social media are rumors. Rockstar traditionally reveals casting close to or after release, so expect to wait.

Will there be a third protagonist?

Nothing official suggests one. All Rockstar materials describe a two-protagonist story. A third playable character would be pure speculation at this point.

Do Jason and Lucia have special abilities?

Unconfirmed. GTA 5 gave each protagonist a special ability, and fans hope for something similar, but Rockstar has said nothing about abilities, stats, or skill systems for GTA 6.
