GTA 6 Crossplay, Cross-Save, and PC: The Honest Status Guide

Last checked: October 14, 2026 | Fan guide, not affiliated with Rockstar Games

Two of the most asked questions about GTA 6 have the least official answers: will it support crossplay, and when is the PC version coming? Rockstar has confirmed the launch platforms and the November 19, 2026 release date, but on crossplay, cross-save, and PC, the company has stayed quiet. This guide separates what is confirmed from what is rumor, so you know exactly where things stand.

For the running technical breakdown as news develops, the GTA 6 crossplay status page is updated whenever Rockstar or Take-Two says anything new.

Launch platforms: confirmed

Rockstar confirmed that GTA 6 launches on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S on November 19, 2026. That date was set after the second delay, announced November 6, 2025, which moved the game from its earlier May 26, 2026 window. No other platforms have been confirmed for launch day.

The current generation only approach makes sense: GTA 6 is built for modern hardware, and Rockstar has made no mention of PS4 or Xbox One versions. If you are still on last-gen hardware, you will need to upgrade to play at launch.

Crossplay: not confirmed

Rockstar has not announced crossplay for GTA 6, neither for the story mode nor for the next iteration of GTA Online. In GTA 5 and GTA Online, PlayStation, Xbox, and PC players have always been kept in separate pools, and Rockstar has never added cross-platform play to the series.

That history makes crossplay far from guaranteed. On the other hand, crossplay is now standard in most big multiplayer games, and fan demand is enormous. Until Rockstar confirms or denies it, treat every crossplay claim as a rumor. Cross-save, carrying progress between platforms, is in the same boat: unannounced and unconfirmed.

The PC version: unannounced but widely expected

Here is the confirmed part: Rockstar has not announced a PC version of GTA 6. The PC platform is absent from all official announcements. Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick has said in interviews that Rockstar tends to announce platforms in due course, which fans read as a hint, but it is not a confirmation.

Rumor, not confirmed: industry watchers widely expect a PC release 6 to 18 months after the console launch, based on Rockstar's history. GTA 5 took about 18 months to reach PC, and Red Dead Redemption 2 took about a year. Reports of a PC version in development circulate regularly, but none come from Rockstar itself.

The honest read: a PC version is very likely given Rockstar's track record and the size of the PC market, but the date, features, and even existence remain unconfirmed. Keep an eye on the GTA 6 PC release date tracker for the latest confirmed news and clearly labeled rumors.

What this means for you at launch

If you play on PS5, expect to play with PS5 players. If you play on Xbox Series X|S, expect Xbox players. Plan your launch-day crew around your own platform, because counting on crossplay that Rockstar has not promised is a good way to be disappointed.

PC players face a wait of unknown length. Rockstar's PC ports have historically been worth it, with better visuals and modding communities, but patience is required. The $79.99 price point reported by some outlets is also unconfirmed by Rockstar, so budget with that in mind and wait for official pricing.

Will GTA 6 have crossplay at launch?

Unknown. Rockstar has neither confirmed nor denied crossplay for GTA 6. Given the series has never had it, do not assume it will be there on day one.

Is cross-save confirmed?

No. Rockstar has said nothing about carrying progress between platforms. GTA Online character transfers existed in limited form in the past, but nothing comparable has been announced for GTA 6.

When is GTA 6 coming to PC?

There is no confirmed PC release date. Rockstar has not announced a PC version at all. Based on the studio's history with GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2, a PC release a year or more after consoles is plausible, but that is informed speculation, not a fact.

Will the PC version cost $79.99?

The $79.99 figure has been reported by some outlets but never confirmed by Rockstar or Take-Two. Pre-orders are not open as of October 2026, and no official price exists yet. Treat pricing reports as rumors until Rockstar announces them.
